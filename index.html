International Workshop

Security for AI Systems and AI for Systems Security

AISEC brings together researchers, practitioners, and industry experts working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. The workshop welcomes contributions on securing AI-enabled systems as well as using AI to strengthen software, network, cloud, edge, and cyber-physical system security.

Call for Papers

Submit original work on AI security and AI-driven system security.

AISEC invites high-quality submissions presenting novel research results, practical experiences, tools, datasets, benchmarks, and emerging challenges related to the secure design, deployment, operation, and evaluation of AI systems and AI-enabled security solutions.

Scope

The workshop focuses on two complementary directions: security for AI systems, including robustness, privacy, trustworthiness, and secure deployment; and AI for systems security, including intelligent methods for detecting, preventing, explaining, and responding to threats in modern computing environments.

Submissions should clearly describe the problem, method, evaluation, and expected contribution to the AI and security communities.

Topics of Interest

Adversarial attacks and defenses for AI systems
Privacy-preserving, trustworthy, and explainable AI security
Secure deployment of AI in cloud, edge, IoT, and cyber-physical systems
AI-based intrusion, anomaly, malware, and vulnerability detection
LLM and agentic AI security, safety, and misuse prevention
Security monitoring, incident response, and threat intelligence using AI
Datasets, benchmarks, tools, and evaluation methods for AI security
Human, organizational, and governance aspects of secure AI systems
