International Workshop on Security for AI Systems and AI for Systems Security

AISEC 2026 is organized with the 2026 International Conference on Next Generation AI Systems (NGEN-AI 2026), taking place on 1–4 September 2026 in Trento, Italy, and online over Zoom.

The workshop brings together researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity, covering both security for AI systems and AI-based methods for strengthening the security of modern software, network, cloud, edge, IoT, and cyber-physical systems.

Call for Papers

Submit original work on AI security and AI-driven systems security.

AISEC 2026 invites high-quality submissions presenting novel research results, practical experiences, tools, datasets, benchmarks, and emerging challenges related to the secure design, deployment, operation, and evaluation of AI systems and AI-enabled security solutions.

Scope

The workshop focuses on two complementary directions: security for AI systems, including robustness, privacy, trustworthiness, and secure deployment; and AI for systems security, including intelligent methods for detecting, preventing, explaining, and responding to threats in modern computing environments.

Submissions should clearly describe the problem, method, evaluation, and contribution to the AI and security communities. Work addressing trustworthy, responsible, and sustainable AI systems is especially welcome.

Accepted workshop papers will be included in the NGEN-AI 2026 main proceedings, subject to registration, presentation, and publisher requirements.

Topics of Interest

Adversarial attacks and defenses for AI systems
Privacy-preserving, trustworthy, and explainable AI security
Security of LLMs, generative AI, and agentic AI systems
Secure deployment of AI in cloud, edge, IoT, and cyber-physical systems
AI-based intrusion, anomaly, malware, and vulnerability detection
AI for security monitoring, incident response, and threat intelligence
Federated and privacy-preserving intelligence for cybersecurity
Datasets, benchmarks, tools, and evaluation methods for AI security
Human, organizational, and governance aspects of secure AI systems
